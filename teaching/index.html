teaching

Courses I teach and have taught, and the interactive labs I built for them.

MBA 60310: Decision Modeling and Analytics · University of Notre Dame

Interactive labs

Browser-based labs that students use alongside class. They follow the order of the course, from building a first spreadsheet model to simulating under uncertainty. Nothing to install or sign into.

1
Spreadsheet modeling

Starbucks Modeling Lab

Name the pieces of the Starbucks procurement model, write its formulas, and run what-if tables on price and supply.

Open lab
2
Linear optimization

LP Formulation Lab

Choose decisions, objective and constraints step by step, solve the model you built, and trace a Pareto frontier.

Open lab
3
Linear optimization

LP Geometry Lab

Watch the feasible region and the optimal corner move as the data change, and connect it to Solver's sensitivity report.

Open lab
4
Integer optimization

Logical Constraints Lab

Write either-or, if-then and at-least-k rules with binary variables, and check them against every combination.

Open lab
5
Integer optimization

Logical Constraints Practice

43 multiple-choice questions on binary constraints, with an explanation for every answer.

Open lab

Simulation

Monte Carlo Lab

From RAND() to @RISK: simulate a scooter business, see the flaw of averages, and compare prices under uncertainty.
